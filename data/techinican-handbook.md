# CellFasion Technician Scheduling & Availability

## Technician Work Schedule

All technicians are assigned a regular weekly work schedule that specifies
the days and hours they are available to take customer appointments.
Technicians may have different schedules depending on their availability and
employment status.

## Appointment Availability

The online booking system only displays appointment times when a technician
is scheduled to work and is available for appointments. Customers can view
the available technicians for each time slot and may select a preferred
technician when that technician is available.

## Technician Breaks

Technicians receive scheduled breaks during their shifts. Break periods are
blocked from the online booking system and cannot be selected for customer
appointments.

## Days Off

Each technician has designated days off each week. The booking system will
not display appointment times for a technician on their scheduled day off.

## Schedule Changes

Technicians may request changes to their regular schedule, including time
off or shift changes. Approved changes must be reflected in the booking
system so that customers are only shown accurate appointment availability.

## Technician Availability

Technicians can be marked as available, unavailable, or on leave. A
technician marked as unavailable cannot be selected for an appointment even
if their regular schedule indicates that they would normally be working.

## Customer Technician Preference

Customers may select a specific technician when booking an appointment.
If the selected technician is unavailable at the requested time, the system
will show other available technicians instead.
