# CellFix Parts, Warranty and Returns

## Replacement Parts

CellFix provides replacement parts for supported phones, tablets, and
laptops. Parts may be sourced as original manufacturer parts (OEM) or as
high-quality third-party replacement parts, depending on the device and part
being replaced. The part type should be recorded in the repair database so
customers and technicians can identify which component was installed.

## Part Quality

### OEM / Original Manufacturer Parts

OEM parts are original components manufactured by or for the device
manufacturer. When an OEM component is available and selected for a repair,
the repair record should identify the part as an OEM component.

### High-Quality Third-Party Parts

High-quality third-party parts are replacement components manufactured by a
supplier other than the original device manufacturer. These parts should
meet CellFix's quality requirements before being used in a customer
repair.

## Common Replacement Parts

### Smartphone Displays

Replacement displays are used for smartphones with cracked, damaged, or
non-functioning screens. The database records the compatible device model
and whether the installed display is OEM or a high-quality third-party part.

### Smartphone Batteries

Replacement batteries are available for supported smartphone models. The
repair record identifies the compatible device, replacement part, part type,
and installation date.

### Laptop Batteries

Replacement batteries may be installed in supported laptop models when the
original battery has degraded or stopped functioning properly.

### Charging Ports

Charging-port replacement is available for supported devices when the
charging connector is damaged or no longer functions correctly.

## Repair Warranty

### 60-Day Repair Warranty

For this sample database, CellFix provides a 60-day warranty on eligible
repair work. The warranty period begins on the date the repaired device is
returned to the customer.

If a problem related to the original repair occurs within the 60-day
warranty period, the customer may return the device for inspection. If the
issue is determined to be related to the original repair or installed part,
CellFix may repair or replace the affected component at no additional
charge.

The warranty does not cover new accidental damage, liquid damage, misuse,
or problems unrelated to the original repair.

## Returns and Repair Issues

Customers who experience an issue with a repaired phone should contact
CellFix and provide their repair or transaction information. The repair
record can be used to determine the original technician, device, installed
part, repair date, and applicable warranty period.

A repair remains eligible for the sample 60-day warranty when:

* The issue is reported within 60 days of the repair.
* The issue is related to the original repair or installed component.
* The device has not received unauthorized repair work elsewhere.
* The device has not experienced new accidental or liquid damage.
